About Woopclap

Smart learning & quiz platform

Woopclap is an interactive learning website designed to help students study smarter. Students can enter a topic or subject and organize their study material into simple notes and flashcards, then create and practice quizzes to check their understanding.

With multiple-choice quizzes, progress tracking, and study resources gathered in one place, Woopclap makes studying more interactive, organized, and engaging, helping students prepare for exams more effectively.
